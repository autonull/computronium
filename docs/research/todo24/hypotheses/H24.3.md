# H24.3 — Registered Hypothesis

**Status:** open

## Statement

ψ-only adaptation improves task-switch speed relative to θ fine-tuning under matched compute on at least one synthetic curriculum.

## Protocol

paired statistical test across seeds with equal episode/compute budgets

## Prior

Pre-registered prediction probability point: 0.6

## Evidence

- Round 1 (X-H24-H243-H24V1, smoke): temporal ψ 0.125 vs θ fine-tune 0.672
  at 1 seed/10ep; θ-invariance proofs held (brier 0.360). **Caveat
  (instrument ≠ registered benchmark):** the ψ arm rode a bare
  `AdaptivePsiReadout` that could not enter `lab.train` — the measured
  0.125 compared a bare-readout probe against a trained system. Do not
  cite 0.125 as a ψ capability bound.
- Round 2 (X-000001, `scratch/todo26_h243_round2.sqlite3`, TODO26 S.3,
  certified quick-tier, 3 seeds × 10 episodes on the composed backbone+ψ
  system): best ψ mode conflict_adaptive 0.729 (threshold reached 3/3
  seeds, θ bitwise invariant) vs frozen_no_psi 0.234 vs θ fine-tune
  0.979 — **AGAINST** under the round-1 rule (ψ beats frozen but not the
  θ-update control at this operating point). The hypothesis remains open
  for other curricula and for speed-based (episodes-to-threshold) rules.
