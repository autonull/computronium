# Trustworthy Results: the Epistemic Ledger

Three objects are routinely confused in the literature. Here they are separate, and separately addressable.

- **Measurement** — a number with provenance: which run, which seed, which artifact produced it.
- **Claim** — a statement over a declared scope, derived from measurements.
- **Gated belief** — a claim that passed its checks. A belief can lose its gate and re-enter as evidence.

<!-- include: diagrams/ceec.svg -->

Claims are property-tested, not asserted: Lyapunov descent for energy-based dynamics, gradient equivalence between each credit rule and its reference, finite-state invariants for plasticity mechanisms.

<!-- notes -->
This is the differentiator. Lead with the distinction between a number and a belief: most dashboards stop at the left of the chain.

The loop at the bottom matters — a gate is not a one-way promotion. When a later campaign contradicts a belief, the belief is revoked and the evidence stays. Point at docs/CORRECTIONS.md as the public memory of that.
